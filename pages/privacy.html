Privacy & Storage Policy

Last updated: 27 November 2026 · Applicable under Regulation (EU) 2016/679 (GDPR) and Italian Legislative Decree 196/2003 (as amended by D.Lgs. 101/2018)

Data Controller: UniSphere Project Maintainer
Contact Email: privacy@unispherelife.me
Source Code: github.com/matteoppet/unisphere (MIT Open Source)

1. Who We Are & Plain Summary

UniSphere is a free, non-commercial, student-built open-source web application for tracking university courses, study session logs, exams, and optional study groups. We process only the information needed to run your workspace and the social features you choose to use. We run zero advertising, zero behavioral profiling, zero sale of personal data, and zero AI model training on your records.

2. Personal Data We Collect

  • Account & Profile Data: Email address, display name, salted PBKDF2 password hash (or Google Sign-In identifier), plus academic profile fields you enter such as degree program, institution, target CFU, and optional student ID (matricola).
  • Academic Workspace Data: Courses, exam dates and grades, study session logs, goals/checklists, quick notes, and saved resource links.
  • Optional Social Data (Squads & Friends): If you join or create a Squad or add Friends, your display name, email address, live study status/subject, weekly study totals, squad feed notes, and friendship link records are shared with those Squad members or accepted friends.
  • Technical Security Data: IP address held transiently in server memory (for up to 15 minutes) to rate-limit email verification code requests, plus standard HTTP access/security logs processed by our hosting and DNS providers.

3. Purposes & Lawful Bases (Art. 6 GDPR)

  • Contract Performance — Art. 6(1)(b) GDPR: Creating and authenticating your account, sending requested 6-digit email verification codes, syncing your academic workspace across devices, and operating Squads & Friends when you choose to use them.
  • Legitimate Interest — Art. 6(1)(f) GDPR: Protecting authentication and email endpoints from spam, brute-force attempts, and abuse through short-lived rate limiting and transport security.

4. Sub-Processors & International Transfers (GDPR Chapter V)

UniSphere relies on the following infrastructure providers acting under Data Processing Addenda and, where data crosses outside the European Economic Area, the EU–US Data Privacy Framework and/or European Commission Standard Contractual Clauses:

  • Google LLC (Firebase Authentication & Cloud Firestore): Account authentication, Google Sign-In, and cloud database sync.
  • Vercel Inc.: Static site hosting and serverless API execution.
  • Resend Inc.: Transactional delivery of 6-digit email verification codes.
  • Cloudflare Inc.: Authoritative DNS routing and edge security.

5. Cookies & Browser Storage (ePrivacy / Art. 122 D.Lgs. 196/2003 / Garante 2021)

UniSphere uses zero tracking cookies, zero third-party analytics, and zero external font or video-embed trackers. To keep the app working smoothly and offline-resilient, we store only strictly necessary technical entries in browser localStorage and sessionStorage:

  • unisphere_workspace_session_email and local workspace cache keys (to keep your signed-in session active and prevent data loss offline);
  • unisphere_theme (to remember your light or dark theme preference without flashing);
  • Temporary sessionStorage keys for in-progress email OTP verification.

Because every entry above is strictly necessary to deliver the service you explicitly request and there is zero tracking or profiling, prior cookie-banner consent is not required under EU ePrivacy rules and the Italian Garante Cookie Guidelines (10 June 2021).

6. Data Retention

Account and academic workspace data remain stored while your account is active. In-memory rate-limiting IP records are discarded automatically after 15 minutes, and verification code hashes expire after 15 minutes.

7. Your Rights (Arts. 15–22 GDPR) & Supervisory Authority

Under the GDPR, you have the right to access, rectify, restrict, object to processing, and request portability or erasure of your personal data:

  • Instant Portability (Art. 20): You can export your entire workspace as structured JSON or CSV directly inside Settings at any time.
  • Access, Rectification & Erasure (Arts. 15, 16, 17): You can edit or clear workspace entries in the app, and request full account deletion anytime by emailing privacy@unispherelife.me. (Self-serve in-app account deletion is also scheduled in our next milestone.)
  • Right to Complain (Art. 77): You have the right to lodge a complaint with the Italian Data Protection Authority — Garante per la protezione dei dati personali (garanteprivacy.it) — or with your local EU supervisory authority.

8. Minimum Age (Art. 8 GDPR & Art. 2-quinquies D.Lgs. 196/2003)

UniSphere is built for university students and requires users to be at least 16 years old across the European Union, or at least 14 years old in Italy pursuant to Art. 2-quinquies of Italian Legislative Decree 196/2003. We do not knowingly collect data from children below those thresholds.
