Questions, answered plainly

Is UniSphere really free?

Yes. UniSphere is 100% free for students, with no paid tiers, no credit card gates, and no advertisements.

Is the project open-source?

Yes. The full codebase is MIT-licensed and open for inspection or contribution on GitHub.

Do I have to join a group or add friends?

Not at all. Squads and Friends live exclusively inside the Squads tab. If you prefer solo tracking, your Dashboard, Courses, Session Logs, and Exams stay completely quiet.

How is my data handled?

Your academic data is synced for your workspace and any Squads you choose to join—never sold, never used for ads, and never tracked with analytics cookies. You can export your full data as JSON or CSV anytime. Read the Privacy Policy and Terms & Conditions.
